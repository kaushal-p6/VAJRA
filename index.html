How Cell Broadcast Alert Transmission Operates Legally & Technically:
• ITU X.1303 CAP Protocol: VAJRA packages alerts into standard Common Alerting Protocol (CAP XML).
• C-DOT Cell Broadcast Entity (CBE): Directly dispatches signed CAP alerts to NDMA's national Sachet engine.
• Carrier-Grade Overrides: Telecom Operators (Jio, Airtel, BSNL) push non-blockable broadcast tones to cell towers in the target geofence without requiring individual app installs.
• Satellite VHF Sirens: Functions over satellite radio links even when mobile cell towers collapse.

Government Cell Broadcast & CAP Protocol Integration

VAJRA functions as an accredited Alert Originator Node under India's National Disaster Management Authority (NDMA) Integrated Alert System (Sachet).

1. Standard XML Schema (ITU-T X.1303 CAP v1.2)
Alerts emitted by VAJRA include geofenced GeoJSON polygons, severity headers, hazard classification, and evacuation directions.
2. C-DOT Cell Broadcast Entity (CBE) Pipeline
Dispatches warning signals directly to targeted cell towers in vulnerable sectors. Cell phones sound a high-pitch warning chime regardless of silent mode or mobile data state.
3. Offline Satellite VHF Gateway Sirens
In mountainous areas where cell towers are destroyed during landslides, local radio sirens are triggered via satellite link.
